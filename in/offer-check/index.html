Why CTC and in-hand feel like two different jobs

Cost to company is exactly that — the employer's cost. Offers routinely fold in the employer's PF contribution, a gratuity accrual you only see after five years, group insurance premiums, meal cards, a variable component quoted at 100% target, a joining bonus with a clawback and the notional value of stock options. A ₹15 lakh CTC rarely means ₹1,25,000 a month in the bank — often under ₹1 lakh once the non-cash lines are stripped out. Freshers and job-switchers discover the gap on the first payslip; this tool shows it before you sign. The money that does leave your salary is not always safe either: EPFO receives more than 15 lakh grievances a year, many about contributions deducted but never deposited.

How the check works

  1. Sorts every line into fixed cash (basic, HRA, fixed allowances), conditional cash (variable pay, joining bonus, ESOP/RSU) and amounts that never reach your bank (employer PF, gratuity, NPS, insurance, benefits). If the stated CTC is larger than the lines, the gap is flagged.
  2. Computes in-hand two ways: certain (variable at 0%) and best case (variable at 100%), after employee PF (12% of basic, capped at the ₹25,000 wage ceiling unless your employer contributes on full basic), state professional tax and income tax under the new regime (standard deduction ₹75,000, rebate up to ₹12 lakh, 4% cess) or the old regime with your deductions.
  3. Explains each line: when gratuity is actually paid, what a joining bonus is worth after tax, why ESOP value is not salary.
  4. Compares with your first payslip and names the usual causes of a shortfall — PF on full basic, TDS on projected income, old-regime deductions not yet applied.

Before you sign: five questions for HR

  • What was the average variable payout, as a percentage of target, in the last two years — and when is it paid?
  • Is PF calculated on the ₹25,000 ceiling or on full basic? (Full basic lowers in-hand but raises your retirement savings.)
  • What is the clawback period on the joining or retention bonus?
  • For ESOPs: number of options, strike price, vesting schedule and cliff, and the last valuation. For RSUs: vesting and tax withholding.
  • Can I have the complete CTC breakup? Any "flexible benefits" or "retention" line should be spelled out.

After you join: check the TDS actually reached the government

Your employer deducts TDS every month, but you get credit only for what they deposit and report. Log in to the e-filing portal and open Form 26AS and the Annual Information Statement each quarter; the TDS shown there must match your payslips. If it does not, ask payroll for the challan details in writing — under section 205 of the Income-tax Act you cannot be asked to pay tax that was deducted from you, but mismatches still trigger notices and delayed refunds, so fix them early.

Frequently asked questions

How much of a ₹15 lakh CTC is in-hand?

It depends on the breakup. With ₹6 lakh basic, ₹1.5 lakh variable and employer PF, gratuity and insurance inside the CTC, the certain in-hand is about ₹1,02,600 a month under the new regime (no income tax, thanks to the rebate up to ₹12 lakh); with full variable it is about ₹1,08,000. Add a joining bonus or ESOPs to the CTC and the certain figure does not move. Enter your own lines to see the split.

Is employer PF part of my salary?

It is part of CTC and it is your money, but it goes to your EPF account, not your bank. You can withdraw it fully only after leaving the workforce or at retirement, with partial withdrawals for specific purposes.

When do I get the gratuity shown in my CTC?

Only after five years of continuous service with the same employer (some High Courts accept 4 years and 240 days; fixed-term employees qualify after one year under the Code on Social Security). It is paid as 15/26 of your last basic per completed year. Leave before that and the CTC line was never money.

Should I count variable pay?

Count it at the historical payout, not at target. Ask HR for the last two years' average. It is also usually paid quarterly or annually and taxed at your marginal rate.

My first payslip is lower than the calculator — why?

The most common reasons are PF deducted on full basic instead of the ceiling, TDS calculated on projected annual income including variable pay, professional tax in your state, or old-regime deductions that only apply after you submit proofs. Enter the payslip net in the options to see which one fits.
