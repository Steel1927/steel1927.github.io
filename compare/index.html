Compare take-home pay across countries (2026)

Enter one salary and see what you would actually take home in 10 countries after income tax and social security.

Legal basis: Each country’s official 2026 payroll rules · ExchangeRate-API rates · Checked 2026-09-24

How the comparison works

Your salary is converted into each local currency, then run through that country's full 2026 payroll rules: the same engines as our individual calculators. The result is converted back so you can compare like for like. Click a country to open its calculator, already filled in, and adjust the details (state, tax class, dependants and so on).

Cost of living, healthcare and pensions differ a lot between countries. Take-home pay is only one part of the picture.

Frequently asked questions

Which country has the highest take-home pay?

For the same gross salary, countries with lower social contributions and income tax leave more in your pocket. Deductions are generally lighter in the US, Canada, Australia and the UK than in Germany, France or Poland, but results vary with the salary level.

Are exchange rates up to date?

Rates are refreshed every time the site is updated. The date is shown under the table.
