Is your super right? Check your payslip

Enter the ordinary time earnings and super from one payslip. See whether it is 12%, why not, whether it was actually paid, and what the gap is worth at retirement.

Allowances, sacrifice, overtime and what your fund received

Legal basis: Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992 · ATO OTE ruling SGR 2009/2 · Payday Super from 1 July 2026 · Checked 2026-09-24. Calculated in your browser. Nothing you type is stored.

More than one in four workers is underpaid super

The Super Members Council's analysis of ATO data (July 2026) found $6.3 billion of super went unpaid in 2023–24, affecting 3.4 million workers — 28% of the workforce — at an average of $1,850 each, and $24.4 billion across 2018–23. Super arrives late or not at all, is calculated on base pay without allowances and loadings, or is reduced by a salary-sacrifice arrangement that is no longer allowed to reduce it. Because payslips show super accrued rather than paid, most people only notice when they open their fund statement.

What the check does

  1. Rate: the Superannuation Guarantee is 12% of ordinary time earnings — from 1 July 2026 called qualifying earnings: base pay, allowances, shift and casual loadings, commissions, performance bonuses, paid leave and any salary-sacrificed super. Overtime, expense reimbursements, termination payouts and parental leave are excluded. Super stops once qualifying earnings for the year reach the maximum contribution base of $270,830 (2026–27).
  2. Diagnosis: if the payslip is short, the tool tests the common causes — 12% of base pay only, the old 11.5% rate, or 12% of pay after salary sacrifice, which has been unlawful since 1 January 2020.
  3. Payment: from 1 July 2026 (Payday Super) employers must get contributions into your fund within 7 business days of each payday. Enter what your fund received over the last three months against what your payslips added up to.
  4. Cost: a shortfall is projected to age 67 at the Moneysmart "Balanced" return of 6.1% a year net of tax and fees (before inflation), so you can see what a few dollars a fortnight really cost.

How to confirm what was paid

  • ATO online services in myGov → Super → Information → Employer contributions: shows what your employer reported and what funds received, per quarter.
  • Your fund's app or statement: contributions should appear within days of each payday under Payday Super.
  • Payslip: must show the super amount and, from 2026, the date it was or will be paid.

If it is wrong or missing

  1. Ask your employer in writing for the calculation and the payment date and reference. Keep the payslips.
  2. If it is not in your fund 7 business days after payday, report it to the ATO with the online tool Report unpaid super contributions from my employer (you need your TFN, the period and the employer's ABN) or on 13 10 20. The ATO raises the Superannuation Guarantee Charge — the shortfall, daily interest from payday and an administrative uplift of up to 60% — and pays recovered amounts into your fund. The ATO generally does not pursue periods more than five years old, so report early.
  3. Award or enterprise-agreement super above 12%, or unpaid wages, go to the Fair Work Ombudsman.

Frequently asked questions

How much super should I get?

12% of your ordinary time earnings (qualifying earnings from 1 July 2026), paid by your employer on top of your salary. On $3,200 a fortnight that is $384; on $80,000 a year, $9,600. It stops once your earnings for the year pass $270,830.

Does super get paid on overtime, allowances or bonuses?

Not on overtime. Yes on most allowances, shift and casual loadings, commissions, performance bonuses and paid annual, sick and long-service leave. Expense reimbursements, unused-leave payouts on termination and parental leave (government or employer paid) are excluded.

Can salary sacrifice reduce my employer super?

No. Since 1 January 2020 the guarantee must be calculated on your earnings before salary sacrifice, and your sacrificed amount cannot be counted as the employer contribution.

My payslip shows super but my fund has nothing — what now?

Payslips show super accrued, not paid. Check ATO online services in myGov for what your employer actually paid. From 1 July 2026 it must arrive within 7 business days of payday; if it has not, ask your employer, then report unpaid super to the ATO.

How is the retirement figure worked out?

The shortfall per year is compounded at the assumed return until age 67. The default 6.1% is the Moneysmart calculator's Balanced return net of tax and fees, before inflation; change it in the options. It is an illustration, not a forecast.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing you type is stored or transmitted.
