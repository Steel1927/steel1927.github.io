How your take-home pay is worked out (2026/27)

Take-home pay = gross salary − Income Tax − National Insurance − pension − student loan repayments. This calculator uses the tax year running from 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027.

Income Tax: England, Wales and Northern Ireland

The Personal Allowance shrinks by £1 for every £2 of income over £100,000, so it is gone by £125,140. That produces an effective 60% rate between £100,000 and £125,140.

National Insurance (Class 1, employee)

You pay 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270 a year, and 2% above £50,270. Your employer pays 15% on earnings above £5,000.

Student loan repayments

Plan 1: 9% above £26,900 · Plan 2: 9% above £29,385 · Plan 4: 9% above £33,795 · Plan 5: 9% above £25,000 · Postgraduate loan: 6% above £21,000.

Pensions

Net pay schemes take your contribution before Income Tax, but not before National Insurance. Salary sacrifice reduces your salary, so you save Income Tax, NI and student loan. Relief at source schemes take 80% of the contribution from your net pay and the provider claims the 20% basic-rate relief.

Figures use the statutory annual calculation. Payroll software working period by period with a tax code such as 1257L may differ by a few pence to a few pounds.

Frequently asked questions

How much is £35,000 after tax in 2026/27?

In England, Wales or Northern Ireland, with no pension or student loan, take-home pay is about £28,720 a year (£2,393 a month): £4,486 Income Tax and £1,794 National Insurance.

What is the Personal Allowance for 2026/27?

£12,570, frozen until April 2028. It is reduced by £1 for every £2 of income above £100,000.

Why is my payslip slightly different?

Payroll calculates tax each pay period using your tax code, so it can differ slightly from an annual estimate. Emergency tax codes or benefits in kind can also change the figure.

Does salary sacrifice save National Insurance?

Yes. In 2026/27 salary-sacrificed pension contributions are free of both Income Tax and National Insurance. A £2,000 cap on NI-free sacrifice is not due to start until April 2029.
