How we calculate & sources

Every figure on this site follows the official rules below and is checked automatically against official examples before each release.

Rules applied

  • Rates and thresholds for employers 2026 to 2027 (GOV.UK) — Income Tax bands, NI thresholds, student loan plans
  • Scottish Income Tax 2026/27 (gov.scot) — six Scottish bands
  • CWG2: further guide to PAYE and NI — period calculation with tax codes — free pay (code × 10 + 9) ÷ periods; K codes 50% limit

Method & verification

Annual statutory calculation (Personal Allowance taper, rUK or Scottish bands, Class 1 NI, student loans). The payslip check repeats HMRC’s period method: free pay = (code × 10 + 9) ÷ periods, taxable pay rounded down, K-code 50% limit. Matched against GOV.UK examples.
