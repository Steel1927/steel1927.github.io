How net pay is computed in the Philippines (2026)

Net pay = basic salary + allowances − SSS − PhilHealth − Pag-IBIG − withholding tax. Mandatory employee contributions are deducted before tax is computed.

Frequently asked questions

What is the net pay of a ₱30,000 salary in 2026?

About ₱26,542: SSS ₱1,500, PhilHealth ₱750, Pag-IBIG ₱200 and withholding tax ₱1,007.55.

Up to what salary is there no income tax?

No withholding tax applies if monthly taxable pay after contributions is ₱20,833 or less (₱250,000 a year). That is a basic salary of about ₱22,700 a month.

Is the 13th month pay taxable?

Only the part of 13th month pay and other benefits above ₱90,000 a year is taxable.
